SpellVeda is a modern Spelling Bee application designed to help users improve their spelling skills through interactive quizzes and learning modes.

Features:
- High-quality Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine.
- Dual Modes: Quiz for testing and Learning for definitions and examples.
- Categorized difficulty levels.
- Fully customizable TTS settings (voice and speech rate).
- Modern Material 3 UI with Dark Mode.
- Completely offline functionality.

Built with Jetpack Compose and Room. Free and Open Source.
